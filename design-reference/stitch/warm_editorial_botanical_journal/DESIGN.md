## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy of a personal daily journal grounded by the quiet certitude of an evidence-backed health log. The aesthetic balances warmth, dignity, and clinical clarity without clinical sterility. It honors physical transitions with composure, rejecting juvenile euphemisms and neon trackers in favor of literary restraint, thoughtful botanical tints, and tactile editorial weight.

The interface evokes an archival notebook: soft porcelain paper surfaces, ink-like deep petrol structures, and intentional botanical pigment highlights. Visual hierarchy relies on confident typographic contrast, deliberate air, hairline dividers, and muted functional color-blocking.

Key style principles:
- **Quiet Dignity:** Visual noise is stripped down. Every prompt and data point is treated with calm consideration.
- **Editorial Archival:** Combines timeless serif numerical pacing with pragmatic, highly legible sans-serif utility.
- **Tangible Paper & Ink:** Muted matte layers replace harsh digital surfaces or glossy overlays.

## Colors

The color palette draws from organic dye baths and pressed botanical pigments, anchored in warm porcelain for zero eye strain during low-light bedtime entries.

- **Primary (`#14383B` / `#18443B`):** Deep Petrol / Subtle Forest. Serves as our primary structural ink for high-contrast typography, primary interactive states, icons, and base line work.
- **Secondary (`#DCEB99`):** Pale Citron. The active check-in pulse, interactive focal points, and completion states. Always set with Deep Petrol text for robust WCAG AAA contrast.
- **Tertiary (`#A94332`):** Terracotta. Reserved strictly for physiological cycle landmarks: period-start markers, ovulation peaks, temperature spikes, or urgent symptom flags.
- **Supporting Note Surface (`#DFCDE5`):** Muted Orchid. Encapsulates personal notes, subjective journaling reflections, dreams, and qualitative mood contexts.
- **Quiet Structural Surface (`#E2E8DC`):** Soft Sage. Delivers subtle calendar matrices, passive grid outlines, unselected segments, and soft secondary card backgrounds.
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F2E9`):** Warm Porcelain. Creates a paper-like ground that prevents display glare and binds the pigment tones into a unified tactile canvas.

## Typography

The type system stages a dialogue between human interiority and objective observation:
- **Headlines & Expressive Numerals (`Newsreader`):** Used for cycle days, primary calendar grid dates, phase banners, and journal section titles. Its fluid italic variations and upright numerals confer literary authority and personal warmth.
- **Functional Interface & Data (`DM Sans`):** Delivers clean optical rhythm for symptom tags, body prompts, medical metrics, timestamps, and input controls.

Implementation rules:
- Calendar numerals always employ serif numerals to emphasize temporal progression.
- Phase indicators (e.g., *Follicular Phase · Day 9*) pair a serif title with an uppercase `DM Sans` micro-label.
- Body text maintains relaxed line heights (1.5–1.6) to guarantee legibility during longer journaling sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a split-page medical journal: structured, proportioned, and disciplined with generous margins that let observations breathe.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin` of `1rem`, `gutter` of `1rem`. Dense single-column flow with full-width horizontal symptom scrollers and pinned lower action triggers.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin-tablet` of `2rem`, `gutter-tablet` of `1.5rem`. Enables side-by-side positioning of the cycle wheel/calendar matrix and the daily symptom logger.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout max-capped at `1200px` content width. Fixed left column for cyclical macro-navigation, central stream for timeline entries, and right-hand sidecar for qualitative logs and insights.

### Spacing Principles
- Minimum touch target distance: 48px footprint across all handheld touch targets.
- Consistent modular rhythm: Multiples of `0.25rem` (4px). Inner component spacing defaults to `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px), while contextual section separations use `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional drop shadows and synthetic skeuomorphic blurs. Depth is conveyed strictly through **tonal paper layering** and **hairline ink boundaries**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Sheet (Canvas):** `#F5F2E9` (Warm Porcelain). The fundamental grounding tone.
2. **Structural Inset / Table Wells:** `#E2E8DC` (Soft Sage). Sits flush with or recedes into the canvas, demarcating calendar tracks, past cycle bars, and unselected modules.
3. **Resting Cards:** `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCFA` with an explicit hairline perimeter: `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.12)`. No shadow.
4. **Active Check-in Sheet:** `#DCEB99` (Pale Citron) paired with crisp ink borders `rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.20)`. Creates physical prominence without artificial elevation.
5. **Note Overlay / Subjective Layer:** `#DFCDE5` (Muted Orchid). Soft matte paper tone for qualitative reflection sheets and open-ended text zones.

### Focus & Floating Elements
- **Modal Sheets / Navigation Bars:** Bound by `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.15)` coupled with a low-dispersal ambient wash (`rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.06)`, 12px blur, 4px Y-offset) purely to detach from scrolling text.

## Shapes

Corner radii balance tactile organic softness with systematic medical composure. Surfaces utilize controlled curvature that feels crafted rather than synthetic.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form controls, calendar date cells, symptom chips, and data badges.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Standard for journal entry containers, insight summary boxes, and metric cards.
- **Sheet Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px):** Reserved for bottom drawers, floating action menus, and cycle phase hero modules.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Strictly used for quick-toggle physiological tags (e.g., *Cramps*, *Spotting*, *Restful Sleep*) and primary action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Check-in / Log Today):** Background `#DCEB99`, text `#14383B`, font `label-lg`. Border `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.2)`. Fully rounded pill or 8px soft rectangle depending on prominence. On hover/press: background deepens to `#CEE085`.
- **Secondary Utility:** Background transparent, text `#14383B`, border `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.18)`.
- **Tertiary / Critical (Period Start):** Background `#A94332`, text `#F5F2E9`, border none.

### Chips & Symptom Selectors
- **Unselected:** Background `#E2E8DC` (Soft Sage) at 60% opacity, border `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.10)`, text `#14383B`.
- **Selected (Physical/Somatic):** Background `#14383B`, text `#F5F2E9`.
- **Selected (Emotional/Reflection):** Background `#DFCDE5`, border `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.30)`, text `#14383B`.
- **Selected (Phase Milestone):** Background `#A94332`, text `#F5F2E9`.

### Calendar Matrix & Cycle Grid
- **Day Cells:** Uniform ratio cells separated by hairline dividers `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.10)`.
- **Today Indicator:** Circled by a hairline `#14383B` ring.
- **Period Phase Track:** Continuous horizontal capsule fill in `#A94332` with high-contrast serif date numbers.
- **Fertile / Ovulation Window:** Soft Sage background with fine diagonal cross-hatching or subtle pale citron dot matrix.

### Cards & Journal Slots
- **Analytical Card:** White base `#FFFFFF`, 12px corner radius, hairline border `rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.12)`. Houses chart sparklines, temperature tracking, and cycle day counters.
- **Subjective Journal Card:** `#DFCDE5` surface with border `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.15)`. Features ruled hairline horizontal dividers for unstructured writing.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Areas:** Canvas-toned with an interior border `1px solid rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.20)`. Focus shifts border to `#14383B` with no external glow. Placeholder in `rgba(20, 56, 59, 0.45)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px rounded squares or circles with a `1.5px` petrol stroke. Active check fills with `#14383B` revealing a `#F5F2E9` check glyph.

### Empty & Populated States
- **Empty Days:** Illustrated with fine botanical or astronomical hairline etchings and a soft prompt in `headline-sm` Newsreader: *"No signs noted for this evening."*
- **Populated Timeline:** Left-aligned vertical thread linked by a continuous `1px` petrol line, with Terracotta nodes marking flow days and Citron badges marking daily symptom updates.
